Why this matters in 2026

The ideas are timeless; the operating model is brand new.

🤖

AI is moving into the ops room

AI agents now enrich alerts, propose incident hypotheses, and investigate outages. Google measured ~10% faster mitigation from hypotheses alone, ~44% faster with auto-built investigation dashboards.

🪜

Autonomy is earned, not granted

The L0–L4 autonomy ladder paces how much control AI gets — gated by transparency, real-time risk evaluation, and progressive authorization. Learn the guardrails before anyone hands you a pager.

🧠

Judgment is still human

Machines nail the known failures and flail at the novel ones — which is where the big outages live. The scarce skill in every production system is still judgment under uncertainty.

🌥️

Cloud-agnostic everywhere

Nothing here requires picking a provider. AWS, Azure, GCP, on-prem, or a laptop — the SLI/SLO/error-budget model and the AI-safety framework work identically everywhere.
